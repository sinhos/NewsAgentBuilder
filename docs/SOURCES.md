Creators supply discoveries and demonstrations; investors supply startup perspectives. Course promotion, revenue claims, portfolio marketing and generic motivation still pass through the relevance/evidence filter. A preferred source does not automatically deserve a main story. Primary documentation and measured results should support consequential technical or career conclusions.

Publicly finding an account is separate from reading its recent posts. Instagram, X and LinkedIn profiles need a connected signed-in OpenCLI browser. Company pages use a separate LinkedIn MCP session. Its current response is a page of text without individually verified dates; the app labels that uncertainty. See [usage](USAGE.md) for connections and manual imports.
